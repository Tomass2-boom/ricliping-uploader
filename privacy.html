Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This policy explains what Ricliping Uploader ("the Tool") does with your information. The short version: the Tool runs on your own computer, has no server, and collects nothing about you.

1. There is no server

The Tool is a desktop program. It has no backend, no database, and no analytics. Nothing you do in it is sent anywhere except directly to TikTok’s own API, and only when you post.

2. What is stored, and where

When you sign in with TikTok, TikTok issues an access token. That token is stored on your own computer so you do not have to sign in every time. It never leaves your computer except in requests sent to TikTok.

The Tool also reads the video files you choose to post. A file you have selected is held on your own computer only until the post finishes, then removed. Video files and captions are sent only to TikTok, as part of a post you started.

3. What TikTok permissions are used, and why

  • user.info.basic — to confirm which TikTok account you signed in with, so posts go to the right account.
  • video.publish — to read which posting options your account currently allows, and to publish a video you have reviewed and chosen to post.
  • video.upload — to place a video in your TikTok drafts instead of publishing it, when you choose that.

No other permissions are requested. The Tool cannot read your videos, your analytics, your messages, your followers, or anyone else’s content.

4. Information received from TikTok

Before each post, the Tool asks TikTok which options your account currently allows. TikTok returns your display name, your username, your profile picture, the privacy levels available to you, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are enabled, and the maximum video length allowed. This is shown on the posting screen and is not stored, logged, or sent anywhere.

5. What is never collected

  • No personal information is gathered, stored on a server, or profiled.
  • No usage tracking, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or cookies.
  • No information is sold, rented, or shared with anyone. There is no third party to share it with.

6. Other people’s data

The Tool only ever touches the account of the person signed in. It does not read, store, or process data belonging to other TikTok users.

7. Your control

You can revoke the Tool’s access to your TikTok account at any time in your TikTok account settings, under connected apps. Doing so immediately invalidates the stored token. Deleting the Tool from your computer removes the stored token along with it.

8. Children

The Tool is not intended for anyone under the age required to hold a TikTok account in their country.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
