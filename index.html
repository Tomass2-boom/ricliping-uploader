Ricliping Uploader

A personal desktop tool for posting your own finished videos to your own TikTok account.

Connect your TikTok account

What it does

Ricliping Uploader is a small desktop application used by a single independent video creator to post finished video files from their own computer to their own TikTok account.

Editing is done elsewhere. This tool exists only for the last step: writing the caption, choosing the settings, and posting — so the finished file does not have to be transferred to a phone and uploaded by hand each time.

How it works

  1. You sign in with TikTok. TikTok’s own login screen handles this — the tool never sees your password.
  2. You grant the tool permission to post videos to your account.
  3. You pick a finished video file on your computer.
  4. A posting screen opens. It shows which account you are posting as, a playable preview of the video, a caption field, a setting for who can see the post, and settings for comments, Duet and Stitch. If the video promotes a brand, product or service, you disclose that here.
  5. You review it and click Post. The video is uploaded and published to your account.

Nothing is posted without you. Every post is reviewed on the posting screen and published by an explicit click. The tool never posts on a schedule, in the background, or on anyone else’s behalf.

What it does not do

  • It does not post anything without an explicit click for that specific video.
  • It does not read, download, or collect anyone else’s TikTok content.
  • It does not post to any account other than the one you signed in with.
  • It does not have accounts, users, or a server. It runs on one person’s computer.

Who it is for

One person — the creator who built it, for their own channels. It is not distributed, sold, or offered as a service to other people.
