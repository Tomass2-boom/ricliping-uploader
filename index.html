Ricliping Uploader

A personal tool for sending your own finished videos to your own TikTok drafts.

Connect your TikTok account

What it does

Ricliping Uploader is a small tool used by a single independent video creator to move finished video files from their own computer into their own TikTok account's drafts folder.

Editing is done elsewhere. This tool exists only for the last step: moving the finished file across, so it does not have to be transferred to a phone and uploaded by hand each time.

How it works

  1. You sign in with TikTok on this site. TikTok's own login screen handles this — the tool never sees your password.
  2. You grant it permission to upload videos to your account.
  3. You pick a finished video file and start the upload.
  4. The video arrives in your TikTok inbox as a draft.
  5. You open TikTok, review the draft, write the caption, and publish it yourself.

Nothing is published automatically. The tool can only place a draft in your inbox. Every post is reviewed and published by you, by hand, inside the TikTok app.

What it does not do

  • It does not publish, schedule, or post anything on your behalf.
  • It does not read, download, or collect anyone else's TikTok content.
  • It does not post to any account other than the one you signed in with.
  • It has no user accounts and no server storing your data.

Who it is for

One person — the creator who built it, for their own channels. It is not distributed, sold, or offered as a service to other people.
